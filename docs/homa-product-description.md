## What Homa Is

Homa is a platform that helps homeowners find, reserve and get the best home service providers. It gives homeowners back **time they wish they had**: a few hours of a trusted helper to care for the home.

Typical jobs are kitchen care, toilet care, living room tidying, laundry folding and ironing, wardrobe organising, and picking things up.

### Who it's for

Homeowners who don't have the time to finish their home chores.

## Where Homa Operates

Homa launches in three neighbouring cities: **Salak Tinggi**, **Cyberjaya** and **Putrajaya**. All prices are in Malaysian ringgit (RM).

Each provider chooses which cities they cover. Search only shows providers who cover the city of the customer's address.

## How Homa Works

Homa sells **a provider's time**. The customer chooses what they want done and for how long, and the price is clear upfront.

1. **Search:** pick the jobs you need, a date and time, a duration (1 to 6 hours) and the number of helpers (1 to 10).
2. **Browse:** see providers who match exactly, plus close matches so you can explore alternatives. A close match is a provider who offers some but not all of the chosen jobs, or who is available near the chosen time.
3. **Choose:** open a provider page to see their jobs, tools, price, availability, cancellation policy and team. You can tap **Chat** to ask the provider questions first; the chat keeps your chosen details and you can reserve from it.
4. **Review:** check the details on the review page and edit them if needed. The same page is used whether you reserve directly or from a chat.
5. **Reserve:** tap **Reserve** to submit a reservation request. You pay by card or online banking (FPX). A card is authorised and charged only when the provider accepts; FPX is paid upfront. You can edit the request until the provider accepts it. If the provider declines or doesn't respond, a card authorisation is released and an upfront payment is refunded in full.
6. **Job day:** you can see who is coming, the provider checks in, does the job, and marks it completed with photo or video evidence.
7. **Payout:** after the dispute window ends without a report, Homa releases the payment to the provider.

### Time

start times are in 15-minute steps. A start time must be at least the **minimum notice** from now and no later than the **booking horizon**. Duration is in whole hours, from 1 to 6 hours, within the provider's own minimum and maximum. Bookable times come from each provider's own calendar.

### Jobs and tools

Homa sets the **job list** and its groups, such as kitchen or living room, and the **tool list**, the same way it sets the policies and the policy options. Each provider chooses which jobs they offer and which tools they bring from these lists. Because every provider uses the same lists, search can match jobs exactly, and the provider page can show which tools from the list a provider does not bring.

### Helpers

each provider sets the minimum and maximum number of helpers per booking. The maximum cannot be more than the provider's verified team size, so the provider can always name everyone who is coming. The customer picks any number in that range. For example, a provider with a minimum of 1 and a team of 4 can be booked with 1, 2, 3 or 4 helpers.

## Pricing

Each provider sets:
- the minimum and maximum number of helpers per booking
- a flat hourly rate per helper
- minimum and maximum hours, within Homa's range of 1 to 6 hours
- how the travel fee works: either a **separate flat travel fee**, or **already included in the price**
- the cities they cover

The **hours fee** is the hourly rate per helper × the number of helpers × the hours. The **provider price** is the hours fee plus the travel fee when it is charged separately.

Homa adds a **service fee** of 15% of the provider price, paid by the customer. The **total** the customer pays is the provider price plus the service fee. The provider receives the full provider price, and the service fee is how Homa earns.

There are no quotes. Choosing fewer jobs within a booking does not change the price, because you are buying the provider's time. Providers bring their own tools.

## Reservation Lifecycle

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | The customer's card is authorised, or the customer has paid upfront, and is waiting for the provider to respond |
| **Reserved** | The provider accepted the request |
| **Declined** | The provider declined |
| **Expired** | The provider did not respond in time |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by the customer or the provider, or ended as a no-show |
| **Disputed** | A report was filed, a customer challenged a no-show, or the provider missed the completion deadline, and it is waiting for Homa's review |
| **Completed** | The final status of a job: the dispute window ended with no report, or Homa resolved the review |

A reservation stays **Reserved** through the job and the dispute window. If a report is filed, or the provider misses the completion deadline, it becomes **Disputed**. **Completed** is the last status, reached when the dispute window ends with no report or when Homa resolves a review. A challenged customer no-show is the one exception: after Homa's review it ends **Cancelled**, whether Homa upholds the no-show or refunds the customer in full. The provider marking the job completed with evidence is an action and does not change the status.

Payment has its own states, separate from the reservation status: **authorised** (card only: checked and nothing charged yet), **held** (paid and held by Homa), **released** and **refunded**.

## Policies

Homa owns the policies that protect both customers and providers. Providers do not set them; the only thing a provider chooses is their cancellation policy, from options Homa offers. The values these policies use are listed under **Homa-Set Values**.

### Payment Protection

- How the customer pays depends on the method:
  - **Card:** authorised when submitting the request and charged only when the provider accepts. A declined, expired or cancelled request releases the authorisation, so nothing is refunded because nothing was charged.
  - **Online banking (FPX):** paid in full when submitting the request, because FPX cannot be authorised first.
  - Card and FPX are the only payment methods, and each reservation is paid with one of them.
- Homa holds every charged payment until it is released or refunded.
- The payment is released to the provider when the dispute window ends without a report. The window runs until after the provider submits job completion, so the customer always has time to review the finished work.
- A report stops the release until Homa's review is done.
- A customer cancellation fee is released to the provider straight away.
- A customer no-show fee is released when the **no-show report window** ends without the customer reporting it. If the customer reports it, Homa investigates and decides whether to release the fee or refund the customer.
- Released payments are paid out to the provider per reservation.
- **Refunds** always go back to the original payment method. They take as long as the bank needs; for FPX this can be a week or more, so the app shows the customer the refund status and the expected timing.
- The service fee follows what the customer finally pays the provider: it is 15% of that amount. A full refund refunds the whole service fee; a partial refund refunds the matching share.
- All payments for a reservation must stay on Homa.

### Request Policy

- The customer can edit a request (jobs, date and time, duration or helpers) until the provider accepts it. For a card, the authorisation is updated to the new total. For FPX, the customer pays any increase before the edit is sent, and any decrease is refunded.
- A request expires if the provider does not respond within the **request expiry time**, always before the booked start.
- A declined or expired request costs the customer nothing: the card authorisation is released, or an upfront payment is refunded in full, including the service fee.
- The customer can cancel a request for free at any time before the provider responds.
- If the provider cancels a reserved booking, the customer is refunded in full and the provider may pay a fine (see Provider Cancellation Fines).

### Check-in Policy

- **Who is coming:** before the booked start, the provider names the **lead** (the person in charge on site) and the helpers for the reservation, matching the number of helpers booked. The customer does not pick the helpers but sees each one's name and photo, and who the lead is. Homa reminds the provider at the **team reminder time**, and check-in is enabled only once the team is named. The provider can change the team until the booked start, and the customer is notified of any change.
- The provider is responsible for check-in and job completion. The lead must be at the customer's location with the provider app to check in. How a provider runs their team is their own operation; Homa holds the provider accountable either way.
- Check-in is **location-based**. Homa enables the check-in action only when the device is within the **check-in radius** of the customer's location, and the provider then marks check-in.
- Homa uses the device's location only at the moment of check-in, not continuously.
- The customer does not need to confirm check-in. The customer sees the check-in time, and can report a problem (for example a missing helper) through the Dispute Policy.
- The provider can check in from the **earliest check-in** time and must check in by the booked start plus the **check-in period**. Homa records the check-in time, and the customer can see it.
- No check-in by then is a provider no-show. The reservation is cancelled automatically, the customer is refunded in full, and the provider pays a fine (see Provider Cancellation Fines).
- During the check-in period, the customer cannot cancel until the provider checks in or the period ends. This way a customer is never charged for a provider who did not arrive.
- If the provider has checked in and the customer is not there after the **customer no-show wait**, the provider can report a customer no-show. The provider receives the **customer no-show fee**: the travel fee plus 30% of the hours fee. The customer is refunded the rest. The customer is notified and can report a wrongful no-show within the **no-show report window**; Homa then investigates and decides. This is a Homa policy that applies to every provider; providers do not choose it.
- A customer who cancels after the provider has checked in is treated the same way, as a customer no-show.
- The job time stays as booked. A provider who arrives within the check-in period but late is tolerated unless the customer reports it.

### Completion Policy

- The provider marks the job completed, and photo or video evidence is required. There is no automatic completion.
- If the provider has not submitted completion by the booked end plus the **completion reminder time**, Homa reminds them to submit it.
- If the provider has still not submitted completion by the **completion deadline**, the reservation moves to **Disputed** and Homa reviews it. Homa decides on a release, a partial refund or a full refund.
- Evidence is used only for disputes and kept for the **evidence retention period**.

### Dispute Policy

- There is one dispute window, from the booked start until the **dispute window extension** after the later of the booked end and the moment the provider submits job completion. The customer is notified when the provider marks the job completed.
- Homa acts only on reports. The customer and the provider are each expected to file a report if something goes wrong. If no report is filed before the dispute window ends, Homa is not responsible for the issue, and the payment is released as normal.
- The customer and the provider can each file one report per reservation, with a reason, and evidence where available.
- A report is one of two types: a problem during the job (for example a missing helper or missing tools), or a problem with the finished work.
- A report moves the reservation to **Disputed**. Homa reviews it and decides on a release, a partial refund or a full refund.

### Conduct Policy

- A violation is a report that Homa's review upholds.
- Consequences of violations are not part of the MVP. In the MVP, Homa only decides the outcome of each report (a release, a partial refund or a full refund).

### Verification Policy

- Every provider is an individual and is verified through eKYC.
- A provider who adds helpers runs a team. Every helper must also be KYC verified. Helpers have no accounts, so the provider sends each helper an eKYC verification link from the provider app.
- Customers register with their phone number, verified by an SMS code.

### Privacy Policy

Homa follows Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), as amended in 2024.

- **What Homa collects and why:**
  - Customers: phone number and address, to sign in, match providers by city and locate check-in.
  - Providers and helpers: eKYC identity data, name and photo, to verify them and show customers who is coming; providers' payout details, handled by Stripe.
  - Location: only the device's location at the moment of check-in, never continuously.
  - Job evidence (photos and videos) and chat, to support disputes and Homa's reviews.
  - Payment details are handled by Stripe; Homa does not store card or bank details.
- **Consent:** asked at sign-up and at eKYC. Location permission is asked only when checking in. Each helper consents through their own eKYC link.
- **Retention:** evidence is kept for the **evidence retention period**. Other personal data is kept while the account is active and deleted or anonymised after the account is closed, except records Homa must keep by law (such as payment records).
- **User rights:** customers, providers and helpers can see their data, correct it, get a copy, and ask to close their account and delete their data.
- **Sharing:** personal data is shared only with the external services listed below, to run Homa, and with authorities when the law requires. Customers see only what they need about providers and helpers (names, photos, check-in time), and providers see only the customer's name, address and reservation details.
- **Data breaches:** Homa has a breach response process and notifies the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and affected users as the law requires.
- **Data protection officer:** Homa names a data protection officer before it reaches the size where the law requires one.
- The exact wording is checked by a lawyer before launch.

### Scope Policy

- The booked jobs are the scope of a reservation.
- Any additions or changes are left to the customer and provider to agree between themselves, and Homa does not review disputes about them.
- A reserved booking cannot be rescheduled. To change the date or time, the customer cancels under the provider's cancellation policy and submits a new request.

### Damage Policy

- Homa does not compensate damage.
- Homa assists manually when a customer or provider wants to make a police report.

### Cancellation Policy (chosen by the provider)

- Each provider chooses one of three tiers Homa offers: **Flexible**, **Standard** or **Firm**. The tiers are listed under **Homa-Set Values**.
- **Flexible** is free cancellation: the customer is refunded in full when cancelling before the booked start.
- **Standard** and **Firm** have a **fee window**. If the customer cancels within the fee window before the booked start, the provider receives 10% (Standard) or 20% (Firm) of the **hours fee**. The travel fee is refunded. Cancelling before the fee window opens is a full refund.
- The customer can cancel for free at any time before the provider responds to the request.
- The tiers apply only until the booked start. During the check-in period the customer cannot cancel until the provider checks in or the period ends; after check-in, a customer cancellation is treated as a customer no-show (see Check-in Policy).
- When the travel fee is included in the price, the percentage applies to the full provider price.
- No cancellation fee is higher than the customer no-show fee, which is the ceiling.
- The chosen policy is shown on the provider page, before the customer reserves.
- Customers never see the fee only as a percentage. The provider page explains the tier in words (for example, "Standard: free until 24 hours before; after that, 10% of the hours fee"). The review page and the reservation details show the exact amount and time for that booking (for example, "Cancel after Sat 9:00 am: you pay RM36. Before that: free").

### Provider Cancellation Fines

- When a provider cancels or does not show, the customer is always refunded in full.
- A provider who cancels more than 24 hours before the booked start pays no fine.
- A provider who cancels within 24 hours before the booked start pays Homa a fine of **20% of the hours fee**.
- A provider who does not check in by the booked start plus the check-in period, who cancels after the booked start, or who cancels after checking in, pays Homa a fine of **30% of the hours fee**. This is the highest provider fine, so not showing up is never cheaper than cancelling late.
- The fines are the same for every provider. They do not depend on the cancellation tier the provider chose.
- When the travel fee is included in the price, the percentage applies to the full provider price.
- Homa deducts an unpaid fine from the payouts of the provider's next successful reservations until the fine is cleared. Each deduction is at most the **fine deduction cap** of that payout, so the provider always takes home part of every job.
- Homa can waive a fine after review, for example for a genuine emergency or when the customer caused the problem.
- Homa shows each provider's cancellation count on their provider page.
- There is no suspension in the MVP. Homa tracks each provider's late cancellations and no-shows in the admin console only.

## Homa-Set Values

These are the values Homa sets for the policies above. They are the same for every reservation and provider. Providers only choose which cancellation tier applies to them, and their own hours and helpers within Homa's ranges.

| Value | Used by | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| **Service fee** | Pricing | 15% of the provider price, paid by the customer |
| **Duration range** | How Homa Works | 1 to 6 hours; each provider sets their own minimum and maximum within it |
| **Helpers per search** | How Homa Works | 1 to 10 |
| **Minimum notice** | How Homa Works | The start time must be at least 2 hours from now |
| **Booking horizon** | How Homa Works | Up to 30 days ahead |
| **Team reminder time** | Check-in Policy | 6 hours before the booked start, while the team is not named |
| **Earliest check-in** | Check-in Policy | 15 minutes before the booked start |
| **Check-in period** | Check-in Policy | 15 minutes after the booked start |
| **Check-in radius** | Check-in Policy | 300 metres |
| **Customer no-show wait** | Check-in Policy | 15 minutes |
| **Customer no-show fee** | Check-in Policy | Travel fee plus 30% of the hours fee |
| **No-show report window** | Check-in Policy | 1 hour after the provider reports a customer no-show |
| **Request expiry time** | Request Policy | 24 hours, or the booked start if that comes sooner |
| **Completion reminder time** | Completion Policy | 30 minutes after the booked end |
| **Completion deadline** | Completion Policy | 24 hours after the booked end, after which Homa reviews the reservation |
| **Evidence retention period** | Completion Policy | 7 days after the reservation is resolved |
| **Dispute window extension** | Dispute Policy | 1 hour after the later of the booked end and job completion |
| **Fee window** | Cancellation Policy | 24 hours before the booked start |
| **Cancellation tiers** | Cancellation Policy | Flexible: free cancellation. Standard: 10% of the hours fee within the fee window. Firm: 20% of the hours fee within the fee window |
| **Provider late cancellation fine** | Provider Cancellation Fines | 20% of the hours fee, when the provider cancels within 24 hours before the booked start |
| **Provider no-show fine** | Provider Cancellation Fines | 30% of the hours fee, for no check-in, or a cancellation after the booked start or after check-in |
| **Fine deduction cap** | Provider Cancellation Fines | 50% of each payout |

## Key Experiences

### Account

Customers register with their phone number and add their address, which Homa uses to match providers by city and to locate check-in. The account shows the customer's payments and refunds with their status.

### Homepage

A greeting and a search bar to start a search. Below it are shelves curated by Homa, such as "Weekend quick refresh", each driven by a preset search.

### Provider page

- Stats under the provider's name: jobs done, cancellations, and price and minimum hours
- Jobs grouped to be easy to scan, such as kitchen or living room
- Tools the provider brings, and tools they don't, so you know what's missing
- The provider's cancellation policy
- The provider's team: every helper's name and photo, so you know who may come
- A **Verified ID** badge: every provider and helper is verified through eKYC


### Availability

Providers manage their availability in the app and sync with their calendar (Google Calendar or Apple Calendar), so results reflect when a provider can actually work.

### Chat

Every chat belongs to one reservation.
- **Enquiry:** a customer who wants to talk before requesting picks the jobs, date and time, duration and number of helpers on the provider page and taps **Chat**. This creates an **enquiry** with its own chat. An enquiry has no payment and is not a reservation status.
- **Reserve from the chat:** when they have agreed, the customer taps **Reserve** in the chat. This opens the same review page as a direct reservation, filled in with the enquiry details. The customer can edit the details there, then submits. Homa checks the provider's availability again, takes payment as in Payment Protection, and the enquiry becomes a reservation in **Requested**. The same chat carries on.
- Enquiry details are only edited on that review page, never in the chat itself.
- A customer can also request directly without chatting first; the reservation then has its own chat.
- An enquiry that is never requested closes when its date passes. Its chat is archived and stays readable.
- The chat stays open for the life of the reservation, including after the job, for follow-ups.
- **Book again:** from any past chat, the customer can start a new enquiry with the same details.
- In the inbox, chats are grouped by provider, so a regular customer sees all their chats with one provider together.
- Only the customer can start a chat; the provider can reply.
- Every chat shows a reminder that payments and messages outside Homa are not covered by Homa Protection, which includes refunds, disputes and cancellation rules.

## Provider App

Homa has a separate app that helps providers manage their reservations, calendar and offerings, and monitor their income.

### Onboarding

1. **Register** as a provider. Every provider is an individual.
2. **Verify** through eKYC.
3. **Set up your helpers:** by default you are the helper. You can add other helpers and run a team, and then choose whether to include yourself as a helper. Each added helper verifies through an eKYC link you send them.
4. **Set up the profile:** profile name, picture and offerings.
5. **Add a payout account:** the bank account Homa pays out to.
6. **Go live** once everything is set up.

Providers cannot go live until they and their helpers are verified, their profile is complete and their payout account is added.

### Teams

a provider who adds helpers manages them in Homa and chooses whether to include themselves as a helper. Every helper must be KYC verified. Helpers do not have their own accounts in the MVP. Each helper has a name and photo shown to customers. For each reservation the provider names the lead and helpers before the booked start, then checks in and submits completion through the lead on site.

### After going live

- **Reservations:** accept or decline requests, name the lead and helpers for each reservation, check in on the job day, and mark jobs completed with photo or video evidence.
- **Calendar:** manage availability and sync with Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.
- **Offerings:** manage the jobs they offer (from Homa's job list), the tools they bring (from Homa's tool list), the minimum and maximum helpers per booking, the hourly rate per helper, how the travel fee works (separate or included in the price), minimum and maximum hours, cities covered and cancellation policy.
- **Income:** monitor earnings, payouts and any fines being deducted.

## Homa Admin

Homa has an internal admin console used only by the founders for now. It has two jobs: tracking the important information about the platform, and handling reports.

### Reports

- One queue for every case that needs Homa's decision: customer and provider reports, wrongful no-show reports and missed completion deadlines.
- For each case the founders see the reservation details, evidence, check-in record and chat, and decide on a release, a partial refund or a full refund. Chats and evidence are opened only for a case.
- Each decision is recorded with a short reason, and both the customer and the provider are notified of the outcome.
- Fine waivers are decided here too.

### Tracking

- **Overview:** reservations, total booking value, service fee earned, cancellation and no-show rates, and how quickly providers respond to requests.
- **Reservations:** search and view any enquiry or reservation with its full history: status changes, payment states, check-in time and location result, and completion evidence.
- **Payments:** authorisations, customer payments, held payments, releases, payouts and refunds, including failed payments, refunds and payouts.
- **Providers:** verification status of providers and their helpers, offerings, cancellation tier, cancellation count, fines and the record of late cancellations and no-shows.
- **Customers:** accounts, reservations and reports filed.
- **Fines:** every fine with its reason, amount, what has been deducted so far, and any waiver.

### Settings

- **Catalogue and curation:** manage the job list and its groups, the tool list, and the curated homepage shelves.
- **Homa-set values:** view and change the values used by the policies. A change applies to new reservations only.
- **Data requests:** handle requests to see, correct, copy or delete personal data, and record each one.

## Not in the MVP

These are deliberately left out of the MVP:

- Reviews, star ratings and review tags
- Consequences of violations and provider suspension (late cancellations and no-shows are only tracked in the admin console)
- Homa credit, account top-ups and e-wallet payments
- Rescheduling a reserved booking
- Helper accounts
- Skilled trade jobs such as plumbing, air-conditioning or electrical work

## External Services Homa Will Use and Not Develop

Homa will use outside services for these instead of developing them. Stripe Connect is chosen for payments and Kenal for eKYC; the others are still to be chosen.

| Capability | What it does for Homa |
|---|---|
| **eKYC provider (Kenal)** | Verifies the identity of providers and their helpers during onboarding |
| **Payment provider (Stripe Connect)** | Authorises and charges cards, takes upfront FPX payments, holds payments until release, onboards providers for payouts, pays out to providers and processes refunds to the original method |
| **Push notification service (to be chosen)** | Sends reminders and alerts to customers and providers, such as new requests, check-in, completion and reports |
| **Geoservice (open source)** | Handles addresses, locations and distance, including the location check at check-in |
| **SMS provider (to be chosen)** | Sends phone verification codes when customers register |
| **Calendar services** | Google Calendar and Apple Calendar sync for provider availability |
